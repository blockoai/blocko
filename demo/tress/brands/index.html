Brand guidelines

Hair, made uncomplicated.

Tress is everyday hair care for people who want healthy hair without a twelve-step routine. This guide keeps every label, page and photograph plain-spoken, practical and friendly.

Archetype

Personality

Everyman

Supported by Caregiver

Tress talks like the friend who happens to know hair: no jargon, no shame, no pile of products you do not need. Care shows up as useful advice and honest results.

Logo

Logo and usage

Five round-ended lowercase letters in one even stroke sit over a ribbon of three crossing waves: strands being plaited, a tress. The mark keeps the idea in one gesture, a t whose crossbar is a single wavy strand.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter t on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 80 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Use the t mark alone for favicons, avatars and tight spaces.
  • Place the logo on calm areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not detach the braid ribbon from the letters or change its waves.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Plain words first

Say what it does and when to use it. Swap technical terms for what you will notice in the mirror.

Short and steady

Brief sentences, one idea each. Lists beat paragraphs; steps beat theory.

Friendly, never fussy

Speak to one person, kindly and without pressure. Hair is not a problem to be fixed.

We write

  • Wash, rinse, done. Use two or three times a week.
  • Light enough for fine hair, rich enough for dry ends.
  • Start with a shampoo. Add a mask when you want more.

We avoid

  • Revolutionary bond-repair molecular system
  • Fix your damaged hair in 7 days!!!
  • You are doing it wrong
