THE STYLIST BEHIND IT

A working hairstylist with a client list full of recognisable faces, a community-first label and a dog who supervises every launch.

"After years of watching people drown in shelves of promises, I wanted a label my friends could relate to: honest about what a product does, easy to use and a bit funny about itself. Tired of being sold to, I built the brand I would want to buy from."
