Plain words first

Say what it does and when to use it. Swap technical terms for what you will notice in the mirror.

Short and steady

Brief sentences, one idea each. Lists beat paragraphs; steps beat theory.

Friendly, never fussy

Speak to one person, kindly and without pressure. Hair is not a problem to be fixed.

We write

  • Wash, rinse, done. Use two or three times a week.
  • Light enough for fine hair, rich enough for dry ends.
  • Start with a shampoo. Add a mask when you want more.

We avoid

  • Revolutionary bond-repair molecular system
  • Fix your damaged hair in 7 days!!!
  • You are doing it wrong
