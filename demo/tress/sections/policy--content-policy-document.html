We want every bottle you buy to be fresh, genuine and covered by our care team. This sample policy explains how a store can describe who is allowed to resell its products.

Who counts as authorised

Authorised sellers are the store itself and the retail partners it names on a public list. Anyone else is an unauthorised seller, even when a listing looks convincing.

What that means for you

Products bought from unauthorised sellers may be old, tampered with or counterfeit, so returns, refunds and subscription perks do not apply to them.

Reporting a listing

If you spot our products on a marketplace that is not on the list, send the link to care@example.com. This is demonstration copy and does not replace a business legal policy.
