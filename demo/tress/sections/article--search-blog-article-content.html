Cocktailing simply means combining two products in your palm instead of using them one after another. Stylists do it to dial care up or down without adding another step.

Start with your usual shampoo and add a pea-sized amount of a treatment that answers today's problem. Dry ends call for a bond balm. A heavy, flat scalp calls for a clarifying wash. A rough patch of frizz might just need a drop of oil.

Match the mix to your strand thickness.

Fine hair does best with a lighter ratio, about three parts shampoo to one part treatment. Medium hair can take equal parts. Thick or coily hair can handle a masque stirred straight into conditioner.

Two products is the sweet spot. Past that, formulas start to cancel each other out and it gets hard to tell what is helping.

Keep notes for a week.
