Shipping and returns

What we charge, how long it takes, and how to send something back.

Last updated: October 2026

On this page

ProcessingShipping ratesReturnsMissing parcels

1. Processing

Orders are packed within two to three business days. You can edit or cancel an order only in the first hour after checkout.

2. Shipping rates

Standard US delivery takes about five to seven days: free from $50, otherwise $5. Three-day expedited shipping is $15. International parcels arrive with duties and taxes prepaid.

3. Returns and exchanges

Return or swap any full-size item within 30 days of delivery, or 60 days over the holidays. Samples and gift cards are final sale. Refunds go to the original payment method or become store credit.

4. Delivered but missing

If tracking says delivered and nothing is there, wait a day, check with neighbours, then contact us and we will open a claim.
