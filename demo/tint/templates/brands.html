Brand guidelines

Colour that plays well with you.

Tint is playful, clean makeup built around shades you can try, mix and own. This guide keeps every page, label and photograph bright, direct and a little cheeky.

Archetype

Personality

Jester

Supported by Creator

Tint treats makeup as play, not performance. It invites people to try a shade, swap it and laugh about it, while the formulas and the layout stay clear, honest and uncluttered.

Every shade is easy to try and fun to wear, with no rules attached.

Logo

Logo and usage

Four heavy, square-cut capitals with the I drawn as a swatch in the accent colour, over a ladder of four steps that fade from full colour to a light wash: a tint. The mark keeps the T and the first two steps inside a square frame.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the width of the letter I on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 88 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the I and the ladder in the accent colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm, flat areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not round, slant or outline the letters.
  • Do not reorder, recolour or remove the steps in the ladder.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Bright and brief

Short lines, active verbs, one idea at a time. A headline should feel like a swatch: quick to read, hard to forget.

Shade first

Lead with the colour, finish and wear, in plain words. Name the shade before the benefit.

Friendly, never silly

Wink at the reader, not at the product. Humour is light, inclusive and never at anyone's expense.

We write

  • Swipe it, blend it, wear it out.
  • Ten shades, one easy-going lip tint.
  • Fragrance-free. Smudges a little. Looks great anyway.

We avoid

  • The only lip colour you will ever need!!!
  • Flawless, perfect, camera-ready skin
  • Fix your face in 60 seconds

Imagery

Photography

Colour you can see

Flat, bright backgrounds in butter, powder blue and blush. The product or shade is always the loudest thing on the page.

Swatches, close up

Real skin, real swatches, visible texture. Show the shade on a range of skin tones.

Candid and playful

People mid-laugh, mid-swipe, looking away from the lens. Natural light, no heavy retouching.
