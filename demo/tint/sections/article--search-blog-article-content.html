Matching a concealer takes three checks, none of which needs a store. Start in daylight with a clean, bare face.

Check one: depth.

Pick the family that sits closest to the skin on your jawline, not your wrist. Fair, light, medium, tan and deep each cover a range, and the number inside the shade name shows where you sit within it.

“If you can see a line where it stops, go one step closer to your neck.”

Check two: undertone.

The letter at the end of the code is the undertone: C for cool, N for neutral, W for warm, O for olive. Pink or red tones lean cool, golden ones lean warm, and a greenish cast points to olive.

Check three: swatch and wait.

Blend a stripe along the jaw and wait two minutes. Some formulas oxidize slightly, so the right shade is the one that still disappears after the wait.
