How to use it

Cover, blend, set

Step one

Dot where you need it

Place two small dots at the inner corner and one on any blemish.

Step two

Tap, do not rub

Press with a ring finger or a damp sponge so the cream melts into skin.

Step three

Set only if you crease

A light dusting of powder locks it in for oily lids or a long day.
