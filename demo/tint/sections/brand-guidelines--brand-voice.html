Voice

How we sound

Bright and brief

Short lines, active verbs, one idea at a time. A headline should feel like a swatch: quick to read, hard to forget.

Shade first

Lead with the colour, finish and wear, in plain words. Name the shade before the benefit.

Friendly, never silly

Wink at the reader, not at the product. Humour is light, inclusive and never at anyone's expense.

We write

  • Swipe it, blend it, wear it out.
  • Ten shades, one easy-going lip tint.
  • Fragrance-free. Smudges a little. Looks great anyway.

We avoid

  • The only lip colour you will ever need!!!
  • Flawless, perfect, camera-ready skin
  • Fix your face in 60 seconds
