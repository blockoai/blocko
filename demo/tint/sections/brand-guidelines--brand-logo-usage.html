Logo and usage

Four heavy, square-cut capitals with the I drawn as a swatch in the accent colour, over a ladder of four steps that fade from full colour to a light wash: a tint. The mark keeps the T and the first two steps inside a square frame.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the width of the letter I on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 88 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the I and the ladder in the accent colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm, flat areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not round, slant or outline the letters.
  • Do not reorder, recolour or remove the steps in the ladder.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.
