Brand guidelines

Makeup with nothing to hide.

Bare is plain-spoken, editorial makeup for people who want to look like themselves on a good day. This guide keeps every page, label and photograph as honest and uncluttered as the products.

Archetype

Personality

Innocent

Supported by Everyman

Bare believes less is easier and kinder. It strips away the steps, the jargon and the retouching, and says what a product does in one plain sentence. Confidence comes from simplicity, not from volume.

Makeup that looks like you, only clearer, with nothing to decode and nothing to hide.

Logo

Logo and usage

Four blunt capitals drawn with one flat stroke and chamfered corners, like cut paper. The A is left without its crossbar, stripped back, and a single accent square sits where the bar would be, echoing the product tags. The mark is a squared b holding the same square.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter B on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 80 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the square in the accent colour or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm, even areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not round the corners, outline or add a shadow to the wordmark.
  • Do not add a crossbar to the A or move the square.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Plain words first

Say what it is and what it does. Skip the jargon, the hype and the adjectives that do no work.

Founder-to-friend

Write in the first person plural, one person to another. Opinions are fine; lectures are not.

One idea per line

Short lines with room around them. If a sentence needs a second breath, split it.

We write

  • A sheer tint that evens out, never covers up.
  • Two minutes, three products, out the door.
  • Made for skin that already looks like skin.

We avoid

  • Flawless, full-glam transformation!!!
  • Unlock your ultimate radiant potential
  • Fix every imperfection in seconds
