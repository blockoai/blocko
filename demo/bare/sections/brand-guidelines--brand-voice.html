Voice

How we sound

Plain words first

Say what it is and what it does. Skip the jargon, the hype and the adjectives that do no work.

Founder-to-friend

Write in the first person plural, one person to another. Opinions are fine; lectures are not.

One idea per line

Short lines with room around them. If a sentence needs a second breath, split it.

We write

  • A sheer tint that evens out, never covers up.
  • Two minutes, three products, out the door.
  • Made for skin that already looks like skin.

We avoid

  • Flawless, full-glam transformation!!!
  • Unlock your ultimate radiant potential
  • Fix every imperfection in seconds
