This is the five-minute face we put on most mornings: concealer under the eyes, a cream flush on the cheeks, brows brushed up, a single coat of mascara and a tinted balm on the lips.

1. Neutralize and conceal.

Tap the peach corrector on the darkest part of the shadow, then press the brightening concealer in shade 16 over the top and along the inner corner.

2. Flush the cheeks.

Swipe the cream blush stick in soft pink on the apples of the cheeks and blend upward with your fingertips.

“If it looks like a stripe, keep blending until it looks like a blush.”

3. Define the eyes.

Dab the cream lid color in bone across the lid, then add one coat of minimalist mascara, wiggling at the roots.

4. Finish the lips.

Glide the lip treatment in rose from the center outward, then press your lips together.
