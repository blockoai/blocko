How to wear it

Build cover, not weight

1

Start small

A rice-grain amount per eye is plenty; more can always go on top.

2

Tap and press

Use the pad of your ring finger so the product warms and melts in.

3

Blend and build

Sweep outward toward the temple, then layer on any spot that needs it.

4

Add the neutralizer first

Deeper shadows? Tap the corrector on before the concealer next time.
