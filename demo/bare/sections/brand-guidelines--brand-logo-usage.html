Logo and usage

Four blunt capitals drawn with one flat stroke and chamfered corners, like cut paper. The A is left without its crossbar, stripped back, and a single accent square sits where the bar would be, echoing the product tags. The mark is a squared b holding the same square.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter B on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 80 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the square in the accent colour or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm, even areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not round the corners, outline or add a shadow to the wordmark.
  • Do not add a crossbar to the A or move the square.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.
