What is in the can

Short list. Real stuff.

If you cannot picture it growing somewhere, it is not in Fizz. Here is the whole cast.

  • Real fruit juice

    Pressed cherries, citrus and berries give the flavor, not a lab.

  • Chicory fiber

    Prebiotic fiber from chicory root: nine grams a can.

  • Sparkling water

    Cold, crisp and the first ingredient on the label.

  • Botanicals

    A little mint, ginger and lime peel for a clean finish.

What we left out

  • Artificial sweeteners
  • Artificial colors
  • High fructose corn syrup
  • Preservatives
  • Mystery flavors
