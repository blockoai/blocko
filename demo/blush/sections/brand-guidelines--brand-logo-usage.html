Logo and usage

Five lowercase letters drawn in one even, rounded line, with a single pink dot closing the word like a flush on a cheek. The mark is the round b set in a pink disc, with the same simplicity at favicon size.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the width of the letter b on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 72 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the dot in the accent pink or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm, even areas of a photograph.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, outline or add effects to the wordmark.
  • Do not capitalise the letters or move the dot away from the word.
  • Do not recolour the mark disc outside the palette.
