Applications are open for this year's small-business grants, and we are keeping the process short on purpose. Anyone running a community project or a young company can apply in a single sitting.

Who can apply

Founders who are building something local, with a clear plan for how the money would be spent. We read every submission, and each one is reviewed by at least two people on our team.

“The best applications are specific about what a first grant would change.”

What the money covers

Funding can go toward equipment, a first hire or a month of rent. It cannot cover debt repayments or travel. Recipients also get a mentor from our store teams for six months.

Key dates

Submissions close at the end of the month, and winners are announced a few weeks later in a follow-up post.
