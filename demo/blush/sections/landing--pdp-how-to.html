How to wear it

Three ways to place it

Press

Dab with fingertips

Start on the apple of the cheek and pat outward until the edge disappears.

Sweep

Draw it up and out

Follow the cheekbone toward the temple for a lifted look, or keep it low for a sweet one.

Layer

Stack for intensity

Add a second thin coat once the first has set, and swipe any leftover onto lips.
