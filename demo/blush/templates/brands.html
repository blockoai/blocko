Brand guidelines

Colour, kept simple.

Blush is minimal, millennial-pink makeup for people who like colour without the production. This guide keeps every page, label and photograph clean, light and a little bit cheeky.

Archetype

Personality

Innocent

Supported by Jester

Blush believes makeup should be easy and fun. It strips things back to black, white and one flush of pink, then lets a small joke or a wink do the rest. Nothing is hidden and nothing is overdone.

A little colour that feels like you on a good day, with no rules attached.

  • Playful
  • Minimal
  • Optimistic
  • Candid

Logo

Logo and usage

Five lowercase letters drawn in one even, rounded line, with a single pink dot closing the word like a flush on a cheek. The mark is the round b set in a pink disc, with the same simplicity at favicon size.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the width of the letter b on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 72 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the dot in the accent pink or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm, even areas of a photograph.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, outline or add effects to the wordmark.
  • Do not capitalise the letters or move the dot away from the word.
  • Do not recolour the mark disc outside the palette.

Voice

How we sound

Light and plain-spoken

Short, friendly sentences in everyday words. Say what it is and what it does, then stop.

A wink, not a shout

Humour is dry and small: one smile per page. Never sarcasm, never exclamation-mark energy.

Colour first

Lead with the shade, the finish and the feeling. Keep claims modest and the tone upbeat.

We write

  • One swipe of pink and you are done.
  • Sheer, buildable colour for cheeks and lips.
  • Wear it bare, or layer it up if you feel like it.

We avoid

  • The ULTIMATE flawless-face formula!!!
  • Fix your skin with our miracle blush
  • Sophisticated luxury for the discerning woman

Imagery

Photography

Bare and bright

Even daylight, clean backgrounds, skin with real texture. Colour does the talking.

One flush of pink

Product shots on flat grey-white or pink, with one bold shade at a time.

Candid and close

Friends laughing, cheeks and lips up close. Unposed, never retouched into plastic.

Care for skin, then play with colour. Made with the people who wear it.
