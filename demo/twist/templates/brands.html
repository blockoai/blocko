Logo

Logo and usage

Lowercase letters in one round-ended stroke, with the crossbar of each t twisting into a little wave and the i dotted in the accent. The mark is two strands of hair wound into a twist, one in the text colour and one in the accent.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the dot on the i, doubled, on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 72 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep waves and dot in the accent colour or the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Use the twist mark for favicons, social avatars and packaging seals.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not straighten the t crossbars or swap the lowercase for capitals.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Lowercase and light

Headlines are lowercase and conversational, like a note passed across a table. Keep jokes small and kind.

Price and gift up front

Say what it costs, what is free, and who it is perfect for. Value is part of the fun.

Show, then tell

Name the colour, the grip, the number in the set. One concrete detail beats three adjectives.

We write

  • a scrunchie set for everyone on your list
  • free gift at $60, no code needed
  • sleeps soft, stays put, comes in 6 colours

We avoid

  • Premium luxury haircare experience
  • LIMITED TIME!!! BUY NOW OR REGRET IT
  • Fix your damaged, boring hair
