Shampoo bars

How to switch to a shampoo bar without the greasy phase

A two-week plan, a rinse trick and what to do if your water is hard.

By The Journal Team · October 4, 2026 · 6 minutes

Switching to a solid shampoo bar can feel strange for the first week or two. Hair may look heavy or waxy while your scalp adjusts, and that does not mean the bar isn't working.

Key takeaways.

Give it two weeks, rinse thoroughly and use a mild acidic rinse if your water is hard. Most people see the awkward phase fade after four to six washes.

Step by step.

Wet your hair fully. Rub the bar between your palms or glide it directly over the scalp, then massage with your fingertips for a full minute. Rinse until the water runs clear, then add a conditioner bar to the ends only.

“If your hair squeaks, you have rinsed enough.”

Troubleshooting.

Waxy feel? Try a rinse of one tablespoon of apple cider vinegar in a mug of water. Dry ends? Add a leave-in cream. Oily roots after two weeks? Lather longer and use a clarifying formula once a week.

Which bar fits your hair?

Fine hair does well with a volumizing formula, while thick or curly hair usually prefers the nourishing one. Color-treated hair benefits from the pH-balanced colour care bar.
