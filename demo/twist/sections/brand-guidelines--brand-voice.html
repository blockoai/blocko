Voice

How we sound

Lowercase and light

Headlines are lowercase and conversational, like a note passed across a table. Keep jokes small and kind.

Price and gift up front

Say what it costs, what is free, and who it is perfect for. Value is part of the fun.

Show, then tell

Name the colour, the grip, the number in the set. One concrete detail beats three adjectives.

We write

  • a scrunchie set for everyone on your list
  • free gift at $60, no code needed
  • sleeps soft, stays put, comes in 6 colours

We avoid

  • Premium luxury haircare experience
  • LIMITED TIME!!! BUY NOW OR REGRET IT
  • Fix your damaged, boring hair
