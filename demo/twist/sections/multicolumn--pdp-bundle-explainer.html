your wash day

Four steps to curls that stay.

  1. 01

    Cleanse

    Lather the shampoo bar at the scalp only.

  2. 02

    Condition

    Glide the conditioner bar through the lengths and detangle.

  3. 03

    Define

    Rake the cream through while hair is soaking wet.

  4. 04

    Air-dry

    Scrunch, then leave it alone until fully dry.
