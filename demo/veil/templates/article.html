How to apply a skin tint so it looks like skin

Three minutes, two techniques and the one step most people skip.

By The Journal Team · October 4, 2026 · 6 minutes

A tinted serum should disappear into skin, and the way you put it on decides whether it does. These are the two techniques we teach in the studio.

Start with clean, lightly moisturized skin. Dispense a half-pipette into your palm and warm it between your fingers so the pigment spreads evenly.

Technique one: fingertips.

Dot the tint on the center of the face, then blend outward in small circles. This gives a sheer veil and keeps the skin looking like skin. Pay extra attention around the nose and chin, where tint can pool.

“If you can see the edge of the product, keep blending.”

Technique two uses a dense brush to press the same amount into the skin for medium cover. Work in short strokes instead of sweeping, which lifts product rather than setting it.

Finish with sun protection in mind.

The shade looks lighter for the first minute. Wait for it to settle before you decide you need more, and add a second thin layer over areas that see the most sun.
