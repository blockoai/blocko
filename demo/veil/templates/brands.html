Brand guidelines

Skin care that settles in quietly.

Veil is soft, editorial skin and colour care for people who prefer a ritual to a routine. This guide keeps every page, label and photograph speaking in the same unhurried voice.

Archetype

Personality

Caregiver

Supported by Sage

Veil looks after people the way a good friend does: it notices, it explains without lecturing, and it never makes a fuss. Expertise is shown through restraint and clear reasons, not claims.

Care that feels like being taken seriously and being let off the hook, at the same time.

Logo

Logo and usage

Four fine-line capitals sit under a sheer double arc: the veil drawn lightly over the word. The strokes are one weight, round-ended, and take the text colour; only the arc takes the accent.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter V on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 96 px wide on screen, 24 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the arc in the accent colour or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not separate the arc from the letters or change its curve.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Calm, never loud

Short sentences, full stops, no exclamation marks. The page should feel like lowering your voice.

Specific over superlative

Name the ingredient, the texture, the time of day. Skip best, ultimate and miracle.

Warm and grown-up

Speak to one person as an equal. Reassure without babying, advise without judging.

We write

  • Smooths without a sheen. Use morning and night.
  • Fragrance-free and kind to sensitive skin.
  • Start with one product. Add the next when you are ready.

We avoid

  • Shocking results in 3 days!!!
  • The ultimate anti-ageing miracle
  • You need to fix your skin
