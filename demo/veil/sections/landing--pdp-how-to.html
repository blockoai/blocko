How to use it

Three moves, one minute

Step one

Shake and dispense

The formula has no silicones, so it settles; a good shake mixes the pigment back in.

Step two

Smooth on like a serum

Use fingertips for sheer cover, or a dense brush to build toward medium.

Step three

Let the shade settle

Color shifts from lighter to true tone within sixty seconds, a cue to apply enough SPF.
