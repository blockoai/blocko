The routine

Prep, tint, define

Step one

Prep with face milk

A pea-size of cushioning moisture keeps base from clinging to dry patches.

Step two

Smooth on the tint

Fingertips for a veil, brush for more cover; it sets to a soft, skin-like finish.

Step three

Define the eyes

One coat of lifting mascara opens up the face without extra steps.
