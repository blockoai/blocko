Grooming

Shave before or after the shower?

Steam softens stubble, but timing changes how close and how comfortable the shave feels.

By The Journal Team · October 4, 2026 · 5 minutes

Wearing one scent from head to toe is easy. Mixing a few is where most guys go wrong. The trick is to treat your soap, stick and cologne as one layered outfit, not three loud strangers.

Start by picking a family you enjoy. Our five are woody and musky, fresh and clean, warm and rich, tropical and fruity, green and aromatic. Staying inside one family guarantees the notes get along.

Pairings that work.

Citrus over wood is a classic: a lemony bar followed by a cedar cologne wakes up and then settles down. Amber with musk feels cozy for colder months. Fresh mint with a pinch of spice is a good fit for the gym.

“If you can smell it from across the room, you used too much.”

Let the deodorant carry the quietest scent. Its job is to keep company with the others, not to compete with them.

A few quick rules.

Apply cologne to pulse points after your skin has dried. Two sprays is plenty. When you are testing a new combination, wear it on a day with nothing important on the calendar.
