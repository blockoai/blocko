Brand guidelines

Get loud. Get clean.

Lather is bold, playful grooming for people who like their morning routine to make some noise. This guide keeps every label, page and photograph sounding like the same confident, good-humoured friend.

Archetype

Personality

Jester

Supported by Explorer

Lather takes grooming out of the serious bathroom aisle. It jokes, it shows off, and it heads outdoors, but it always names what is in the bar and what it does. The humour opens the door; the substance keeps people there.

Grooming that is fun to use and honest about what it does, from the first lather to the last.

  • Playful

Logo

Logo and usage

Six heavy, blunt capitals with a hard offset shadow in the accent colour, echoing the offset shadows on cards and buttons. Three foam bubbles rise off the T and H: the lather itself. The mark keeps the bold L and its shadow inside a rounded tile, with the bubbles above.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter L on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 96 px wide on screen, 24 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the shadow and bubbles in the accent colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not remove the shadow, recolour the bubbles or change the offset direction.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Loud, never loud-mouthed

Big headlines, short lines, the odd exclamation mark. Energy comes from rhythm and confidence, not from shouting at people.

Joke, then the facts

Land the grin first, then say what is in it and how to use it. Never let a gag hide a scent note or a price.

Friend, not guru

Talk like the mate who always smells good and tells you where he got it. No lectures, no ranking people's habits.

We write

  • Wood, musk and a splash of cedar. Lathers in ten seconds.
  • Your shower called. It wants better soap.
  • Subscribe and save. Skip or cancel whenever.

We avoid

  • Be the alpha everyone envies
  • Revolutionary formula, unbeatable results
  • Real men don't skip the routine

Imagery

Photography

Outdoors, in motion

Trails, water, wood and weather. People mid-action, a little windswept, never posed in a mirror.

Hard light, rich colour

Strong daylight and saturated greens and oranges. Shadows stay crisp, like the offset shadow in the logo.

Product in the mess

Bars and bottles on wet stone, towels and wood, with real water and foam. Close, tactile, slightly imperfect.

Natural bar soap, deodorant, hair care and cologne for guys who like their routine loud and their ingredient list short.
