Education

Meet the experts who review our ingredient decisions

A chemist, a dermatologist, a lawyer and others explain how the advisory panel works.

By The Journal Team · October 4, 2026 · 6 minutes

Choosing what belongs on a shelf is not a yes-or-no exercise. Many ingredients sit in a gray zone, which is why we asked outside specialists to challenge our reasoning before a rule becomes policy.

A cosmetic chemist.

Reviews formulas for stability and checks that a swap doesn't make a product worse. Spent two decades in ingredient development.

A laboratory scientist.

Runs the testing that tells us whether a batch contains trace contaminants, and explains what the numbers can and can't prove.

A dermatologist.

Weighs what irritation and allergy look like in real patients, not only in test tubes.

“We do not want a list that's right on paper and wrong in a bathroom.”

An environmental justice specialist.

Looks at who lives near the factories and mines behind an ingredient and who pays for the pollution.

A sustainability lawyer.

Reads claims such as “biodegradable” and tells us when the law and the science point in different directions.

A packaging engineer.

Explains why a tube, a pump or a wand ends up in the wrong bin and which designs can avoid it.
