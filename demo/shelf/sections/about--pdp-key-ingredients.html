Key ingredients

What each one is there to do

Hydrate

Sodium hyaluronate

Binds water to the surface so skin looks plumper within minutes.

Nourish

Plant squalane

A light oil that softens without leaving a film.

Strengthen

Ceramide NP

Refills the fatty layer between skin cells that keeps moisture in and irritants out.

Calm

Panthenol

A form of vitamin B5 that takes the edge off tightness and visible redness.

Brighten

Niacinamide

Helps dullness and uneven tone fade from view with steady use.

Soften

Allantoin

Smooths rough patches and eases the feel of dry skin.
