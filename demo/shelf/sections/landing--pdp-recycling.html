Recycle your empties

Three steps, ten points an item

Step one

Empty and rinse

Take off pumps and caps, then rinse the glass so it is free of product.

Step two

Sort by material

Glass, paper and bigger plastics may go in your curbside bin; small or mixed parts need a special drop.

Step three

Hand them in

Leave them in a boutique bin or post them with a prepaid label and collect your points.
