Logo and usage

Square cut lowercase letters stand on a shelf line, and the l carries a small cap so it reads as a bottle. The shelf and cap take the accent; the letters take the text colour.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter h on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 88 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the shelf line and cap in the accent colour or the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not remove the shelf line or move the cap off the l.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.
