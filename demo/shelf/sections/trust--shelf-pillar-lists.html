The rulebook

Required and encouraged

A brand must clear every required item to be listed. Encouraged items win it a place in our spotlight.

Product

Required

Full disclosure of ingredients under standard names. Warning before any formula change. Compliance with local law. Stability, preservative and compatibility tests. Skin patch testing on new launches. Heavy-metal, PFAS and asbestos checks where relevant. Batch traceability. Evidence behind every claim.

Encouraged

Factories that follow good manufacturing practice. Accredited labs. Open fragrance disclosure. Sourcing notes. Organic certification.

Packaging

Required

Follow our packaging guidelines. Limit single-use parts. At least half recycled plastic where plastic is used. Disposal directions on pack. Resin codes. Certified paper. Accurate recycling claims.

Encouraged

Join the packaging collective. Offer refills. Raise recycled content further.

People

Required

A supplier code of conduct covering fair pay and safe workplaces. Traceability for high-risk materials.

Encouraged

Third-party labor audits. Programs that support suppliers' communities.

Planet

Required

No animal testing at any stage. A written position on raw material sourcing.

Encouraged

Climate targets. Lower-impact materials. Public reports on progress.
