1. Return window

Items can be sent back within 30 days of delivery. They must be unused or only lightly tested.

2. Fees

Returns by mail cost $5, deducted from the refund. Bringing an item to any boutique is free.

3. Not returnable

Final-sale markdowns, gifts with purchase, gift cards and rewards redeemed with points can't be returned.

4. Refunds

Money goes back to the original payment method about two days after the parcel is scanned in. Shipping charges are not refunded.

5. Need help

Write to care@example.com with your order number.
