How it works

Return your empties in three steps

One

Clean it out

Scrape out product and rinse. Remove pumps and caps where you can.

Two

Choose the right bin

Big glass, paper and plastics 1, 2 and 5 go in your home recycling; everything small goes in ours.

Three

Give it new life

We deliver your empties to partners that turn them into new materials.
