How to use it

Spray, wait, massage

Step one

Shake it up

Ten good shakes mix the powder with the propellant so the spray comes out evenly.

Step two

Spray the roots

Part hair into sections and spray eight inches away, focusing on the crown and temples.

Step three

Wait, then work it in

Give it a minute to absorb, rub in with fingertips, then brush or style as normal.
