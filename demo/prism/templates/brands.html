Logo

Logo and usage

Five heavy lowercase letters with flat cut ends, and a solid triangle for the dot on the i: a prism standing in the word. The mark is the same triangle with three rays leaving it, light split into colour.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the triangle on the letter i on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 88 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the triangle in the accent colour or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on flat colour or calm areas of a photograph.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not replace the triangle with a round dot or change its angle.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Say it straight

Short, lowercase-friendly lines. Name the step, the result and the time it takes.

Bright, never silly

Wit and colour are welcome. Jokes never replace the answer.

Invite, do not instruct

Offer a hair type, a shade, a next move. The customer picks.

We write

  • Three steps. Wash, treat, glow.
  • Find your hair type in two minutes.
  • Subscribe, save, skip any time.

We avoid

  • The ultimate miracle transformation!!!
  • Fix your damaged, dull hair
  • Terms and conditions apply to all offers
