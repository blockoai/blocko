A good blowout starts well before the dryer turns on. Towel-dry gently, work a heat protectant through damp hair, and decide your sections first so you never double back.

Aim for hair that is about eighty percent dry before you start styling with a brush. Drying the last stretch with tension is where shape and shine come from.

Point the nozzle down the length.

Airflow that runs from root to tip lies the cuticle flat. Pointing it upward ruffles that surface and invites frizz, particularly in humid weather.

“Finish every section with a blast of cool air to lock the shape.”

Use a round brush for bend and a paddle brush for sleekness. Roll the brush away from the face for lift, and tuck under for a polished edge.

Seal it before you leave the mirror.

A drop of lightweight oil on the ends and a quick pass of dry shampoo at the roots keeps the style fresh for another day or two.
