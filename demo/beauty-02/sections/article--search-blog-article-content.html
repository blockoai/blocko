The best wash day has a rhythm you can repeat. It starts with the products you reach for and ends with hair that still feels like yours.

Take a minute to notice what your hair needs today. Some days call for more hydration; others benefit from a lighter hand. The goal is not a perfect routine. It is one that gives you a little more ease.

Start with a clean, comfortable base.

Massage shampoo into the scalp and let the water do some of the work. Follow with conditioner through the lengths, then detangle while everything is still soft and workable.

When you have time, add one finishing step. A little oil through the ends or a leave-in conditioner after the shower can help keep texture flexible without turning the routine into a project.

Make the final step your own.

Dry, diffuse, air dry, or style—there is no right finish. Keep what works, set aside what does not, and let the routine change with your hair.
