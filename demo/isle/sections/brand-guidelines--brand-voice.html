Voice

How we sound

Bright and upbeat

Short, energetic lines with room for a holiday pun. One exclamation mark is plenty.

Sensory first

Lead with scent, texture and warmth: coconut, mango, sun-warm skin. Facts follow close behind.

Easygoing, never silly

Wink at the reader, then be clear about what the product does and how to use it.

We write

  • Coconut body wash that smells like a day off.
  • Pack your shower for paradise. Lather, rinse, repeat.
  • Soft, sun-ready skin from the first use.

We avoid

  • WARNING: skin emergency, buy now!!!
  • Clinically engineered anti-dryness system
  • Escape your boring, dull life
