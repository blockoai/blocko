Logo

Logo and usage

Four heavy, round-ended lowercase letters carry a sun for the dot of the i and a single wave line running beneath the word: a small island at the edge of the sea. Letters take the text colour; the sun and the wave take the accent.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter i, dot included, on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 80 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the sun and wave in the accent colour or the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo over calm areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not move the sun away from the i or straighten the wave.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or a low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Bright and upbeat

Short, energetic lines with room for a holiday pun. One exclamation mark is plenty.

Sensory first

Lead with scent, texture and warmth: coconut, mango, sun-warm skin. Facts follow close behind.

Easygoing, never silly

Wink at the reader, then be clear about what the product does and how to use it.

We write

  • Coconut body wash that smells like a day off.
  • Pack your shower for paradise. Lather, rinse, repeat.
  • Soft, sun-ready skin from the first use.

We avoid

  • WARNING: skin emergency, buy now!!!
  • Clinically engineered anti-dryness system
  • Escape your boring, dull life
