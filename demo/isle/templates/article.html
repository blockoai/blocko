Routine

A five-minute routine that still feels like a vacation.

An easy way to bring comfort, glow, and a little sunshine to an ordinary morning.

By The Island Journal Team · October 4, 2026 · 6 minutes

The best routine is the one you want to come back to. It makes space for your face, your schedule, and the way you feel today.

Start with a little hydration and let it settle while you make coffee or choose an outfit. The point is not precision. It is a comfortable base that leaves room for the rest of your day.

Keep the first step generous.

Apply a light layer of care wherever your skin tends to feel dry. Press it in rather than trying to perfect it. A soft, rested finish is more useful than an overworked one.

“A routine can be both practical and a small act of attention.”

Then choose one color you enjoy wearing. A sheer tint, a wash on the lids, or a little balm on the glow can change the feeling of the whole look without asking much of you.

Leave room for change.

Some mornings call for one step. Others invite more. Build a small collection of pieces that work in different combinations, and let the routine be flexible enough to meet you where you are.
