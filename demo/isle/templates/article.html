Sun care

A sun-smart plan for long beach days

Five habits that keep skin comfortable from the first swim to the last sunset.

By The Journal Team · October 4, 2026 · 6 minutes

A day at the water goes better with a plan. These five habits keep skin comfortable from the first swim to the last sunset, and none of them take more than a minute.

1. Apply before you leave the house.

Sunscreen needs a few minutes to settle, so spray or smooth it on at home and again when you arrive. Cover the spots people forget: ears, the tops of feet and the backs of knees.

2. Set a two-hour reminder.

Protection fades with sweat, towels and swimming. A phone alarm is the easiest way to remember to reapply.

“The best sunscreen is the one you actually reapply.”

3. Mix textures.

Mist the body, stick the face and carry a compact for touch-ups. Different formats make it more likely you will keep going all afternoon.

4. Rinse and replenish.

Finish the day with a gel wash and a heavy butter. Coconut-based moisturizers help calm that tight, sun-warm feeling.

5. Store it cool.

Heat shortens a bottle's life. Keep sunscreen in the shade, never in a parked car.
