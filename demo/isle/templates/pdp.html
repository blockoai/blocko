Step one

Shake it

A few firm shakes mix the shimmer into the formula.

Step two

Spray until it glistens

Hold six inches away and coat each area evenly; a sheen means enough product.

Step three

Smooth and set a timer

Spread it with your palm, avoid the face, and reapply when two hours pass.
