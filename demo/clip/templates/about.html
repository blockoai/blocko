Our story

Two relatives, one drawer of clips.

We began by hand-finishing a few accessories for friends. Today the drawer holds clips, scrunchies, brushes and a small hair-care line, sold online and through large retailers.

From the founders

“We wanted accessories that felt like a treat, and hair care that smelled like one.”

A short note from the two relatives behind the label about how a hobby became a business.

What we believe

Playful, kind and made well

01

Bold and effortless

Accessories should invite you to try something, then stay out of your way.

02

Made for every hair type

Clips grip thick curls and fine strands; care formulas are tested across textures.

03

Vegan and cruelty-free

No animal ingredients, no animal testing, and a third-party certification to prove it.

Materials

What our pieces are made from

01

Plant-based acetate

Clips are cut and polished by hand from a biodegradable material.

02

Bamboo handles

Brush bases are bamboo, with bristles chosen to be gentle on the scalp.

03

Upcycled cotton

Everyday scrunchies are sewn locally from leftover fabric.

04

Charmeuse silk

Our silk bands are cut from smooth French charmeuse.

  3. 2022

    Hair care arrives

    A scented mist opens the door to masks, oils and styling.

  4. Today

    In stores nationwide

    The line reaches department stores and a major beauty chain.
