Style

A five-minute style that still feels like you.

An uncomplicated way to bring comfort, color, and a little fun to an ordinary morning.

By The Journal Team · October 4, 2026 · 6 minutes

The best hair routine is the one you want to come back to. It makes space for your hair, your schedule, and the way you feel today.

Start with a quick brush-through while you make coffee or choose an outfit. The point is not precision. It is a comfortable base that leaves room for the rest of your day.

Keep the first step generous.

Gather your hair wherever it likes to sit. Twist it rather than trying to perfect it. A soft, slightly undone look is more useful than an overworked one.

“A hair routine can be both practical and a small act of attention.”

Then choose one color you enjoy wearing. A pastel clip, a satin scrunchie, or a pearl pin can change the feeling of the whole look without asking much of you.

Leave room for change.

Some mornings call for one clip. Others invite more. Build a small collection of pieces that work in different combinations, and let your style be flexible enough to meet you where you are.
