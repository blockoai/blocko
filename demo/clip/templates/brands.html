Brand guidelines

Little clips, big personality.

Clip makes playful hair accessories for people who treat a hair day as a small, happy decision. This guide keeps every page, label and photograph light, friendly and a little bit cheeky.

Logo

Logo and usage

Four round, even-weight lowercase letters, friendly and open, with a small heart pin dotting the i. The mark is a claw clip seen from the front: an arch, a row of teeth and the same heart. Strokes take the text colour; only the heart takes the accent.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter c on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 64 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the heart in the accent colour or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not swap the heart for another shape or move it off the i.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Light and cheerful

Short, upbeat lines with a smile in them. One playful idea per sentence, never a pile of jokes.

Say it simply

Name the shape, size and finish: big claw clip, satin scrunchie, pearl snap. Skip jargon and hype.

Friend, not salesperson

Talk like someone sharing a find. Invite, never pressure.

We write

  • Meet the claw clip that holds on all day.
  • Pearl snaps, two in a pack. Mix them up.
  • Pick a colour, pin it, go.

We avoid

  • THE BEST CLIPS EVER!!! BUY NOW!!!
  • Revolutionary hair-hold technology
  • Your hair is a mess. We fix it.

Imagery

Photography

Bright and soft

Clean daylight, pastel grounds, no heavy shadows. Colours stay true and cheerful.

Accessories up close

Clips, scrunchies and ribbons shown big, on hair or flat on a plain surface, with room to breathe.

Real and unposed

Everyday people mid-laugh, mid-twist, mid-style. Candid beats perfect.
