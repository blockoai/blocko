Using the duo

Mask first, mist after

Step one

Mask for ten minutes

Work through towel-dried lengths, leave for ten minutes or overnight, then rinse.

Step two

Mist the ends

Hold the bottle at arm's length and spritz over the lengths and the nape.

Step three

Clip it up

Twist and pin with a jumbo clip. The scent lingers until bedtime.
