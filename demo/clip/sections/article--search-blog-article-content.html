She teaches Pilates by day and parties by night, and her hair has to survive both. We asked for the habits behind the effortless knot.

What is your go-to hairstyle?

A low twist pinned with the biggest clip I own. It takes thirty seconds and never falls out, even after a class.

The accessory you cannot leave the house without?

A silk scrunchie on my wrist. It is a backup for the clip and doubles as a pop of colour.

“If it holds my hair, matches my outfit and smells a little like dessert, it is staying in my bag.”

A style icon you keep returning to?

Anyone who looks like they threw their hair up in a minute and still looked finished. That is the whole goal.

Your favorite night out?

Dinner with friends, then a walk home with the clip in my hand and my hair down.
