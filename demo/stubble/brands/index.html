Brand guidelines

Shave well. Skip the lecture.

Stubble is straight-talking shaving and grooming for men who want a good result in five minutes. This guide keeps every page, label and photograph clear, practical and a little dry.

Archetype

Personality

Regular Guy

Supported by Sage

Stubble talks like the friend who actually knows which razor to buy. It explains the product plainly, backs it with reasons, and saves the humour for the moments that earn it. Nothing is macho, nothing is precious.

A close, comfortable shave and an honest answer, without the sales pitch.

  • Practical

Logo

Logo and usage

Seven square-cut capitals with one round-bottomed U and two round-bowled Bs. Beneath them a rule of short bristles gives way to a clean straight line: grown out on the left, shaved on the right. Letters take the text colour; the bristles and rule take the action colour.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter S on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 96 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the bristles and rule in the action colour or the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not remove the rule or straighten the bristles into a pattern.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Plain first

Say what it does in the first sentence. Short words, active verbs, no jargon unless it helps.

Dry, not loud

A light joke is welcome; a shout is not. Humour never sits on top of a price, a warning or a how-to.

Show the proof

Give the blade count, the weeks it lasts, the trial length. A fact beats an adjective.

We write

  • Five blades, one pass. Replacement heads arrive before you need them.
  • Try it for 30 days. Not for you? Send it back.
  • Apply, wait a minute, shave with the grain.

We avoid

  • Unleash your inner alpha!
  • The most extreme shave known to man
  • Real men don't get razor burn
