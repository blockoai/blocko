Voice

How we sound

Plain first

Say what it does in the first sentence. Short words, active verbs, no jargon unless it helps.

Dry, not loud

A light joke is welcome; a shout is not. Humour never sits on top of a price, a warning or a how-to.

Show the proof

Give the blade count, the weeks it lasts, the trial length. A fact beats an adjective.

We write

  • Five blades, one pass. Replacement heads arrive before you need them.
  • Try it for 30 days. Not for you? Send it back.
  • Apply, wait a minute, shave with the grain.

We avoid

  • Unleash your inner alpha!
  • The most extreme shave known to man
  • Real men don't get razor burn
