Why the shave feels different

Pivot

A head that follows you

The cartridge rocks forward and back so the blades keep contact around the chin and the neck.

Blades

Five blades, stepped

Each blade sits slightly higher than the one before, so hair is cut gradually and tugging drops.

Handle

Weight in the right place

A heavier metal body lets the razor glide on its own instead of being pressed into skin.
