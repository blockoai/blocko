Meet the peptide glow serum

A short film on what it does and where it sits in a routine.

By The Journal Team · October 4, 2026 · 3 minutes

If you have ever stood in front of a shelf of serums wondering which one is for you, this film is for you. We walk through what the peptide glow serum does and exactly where it fits.

Begin with a clean face. Spritz or pat on your prep essence while skin is still slightly damp, because that thin layer of water is what the serum will hold onto.

What to expect from the first week.

Skin feels cushiony right after the first use. Over several days, the surface looks smoother and more even, with a gentle shine that comes from hydration and not from oil.

“If it feels sticky, you used too much. Two pumps is plenty.”

The second half of the film is about layering: serum first, moisturizer second, sunscreen last in the morning. At night, finish with a thin balm over dry areas.

Little size, same formula.

The smaller bottle holds exactly the same formula and slips into a gym bag. Many people keep a little one at work and a full size at home.
