Brand guidelines

Quiet skincare for skin that is getting on with its day.

Dew is ultra-minimal skincare: a few honest products, a pared-back shelf and a lot of white space. This guide keeps every page, label and photograph as clear and unfussy as a morning drop of water.

Archetype

Personality

Innocent

Supported by Sage

Dew believes skincare can be simple. It removes the extras, names every ingredient and lets the products do the talking. Knowledge is offered plainly and only when it helps.

Fewer steps, nothing hidden, skin that feels like itself again.

  • Clear

Logo

Logo and usage

Three lowercase letters drawn in one round-ended line, with a single drop resting where the i-dot would be, above the peak of the w. The letters take the text colour; only the drop takes the accent.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter e on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 80 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the drop in the accent colour or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Always set the name in lowercase, exactly as drawn.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not move the drop or add more drops.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Plain and lowercase

Short words, short lines, headings in lowercase. Sound like a note from a friend, not an advert.

Say what is inside

Name the ingredient and the job it does. If we cannot explain it in a sentence, it is not in the bottle.

Never push

Offer the next step, do not demand it. No countdowns, no fear, no exclamation marks.

We write

  • One serum, one cream, one sunscreen. That is the routine.
  • Hyaluronic acid and glycerin. Nothing else needed.
  • Use it morning and night, or only when you remember.

We avoid

  • Transform your skin in 7 days!!!
  • Unlock the secret to flawless, glass-like skin
  • Don't miss out, only 2 left

Imagery

Photography

Clean and bright

Even daylight, pale grounds, true colour. Skin keeps its texture and a little dewy shine.

One thing at a time

A single bottle, jar or hand. Generous empty space and no props that need explaining.

Bare and natural

Fresh faces, little make-up, unposed. Moments after washing rather than before a party.

Quiet skincare for skin that is simply getting on with its day.
