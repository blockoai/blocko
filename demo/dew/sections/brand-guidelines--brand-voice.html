Voice

How we sound

Plain and lowercase

Short words, short lines, headings in lowercase. Sound like a note from a friend, not an advert.

Say what is inside

Name the ingredient and the job it does. If we cannot explain it in a sentence, it is not in the bottle.

Never push

Offer the next step, do not demand it. No countdowns, no fear, no exclamation marks.

We write

  • One serum, one cream, one sunscreen. That is the routine.
  • Hyaluronic acid and glycerin. Nothing else needed.
  • Use it morning and night, or only when you remember.

We avoid

  • Transform your skin in 7 days!!!
  • Unlock the secret to flawless, glass-like skin
  • Don't miss out, only 2 left
