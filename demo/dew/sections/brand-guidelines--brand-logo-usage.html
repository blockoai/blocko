Logo and usage

Three lowercase letters drawn in one round-ended line, with a single drop resting where the i-dot would be, above the peak of the w. The letters take the text colour; only the drop takes the accent.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter e on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 80 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the drop in the accent colour or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Always set the name in lowercase, exactly as drawn.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not move the drop or add more drops.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.
