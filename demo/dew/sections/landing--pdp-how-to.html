Get the look

dot, tap, blot

Step one

Dot on the apples

One small dot per cheek is enough for a flush; go for two for more.

Step two

Tap outward

Use warm fingertips to tap toward the temples. Patting melts the cream into skin.

Step three

Echo on the lips

Press leftover color onto the lips and blot for a matching, tea-stained tint.
