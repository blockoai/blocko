A face stick has three jobs: shaping, warmth and light. The trick is putting each in its own zone so nothing muddies.

Start on bare or lightly moisturized skin. Warm the stick against the back of your hand so it glides instead of dragging.

Zone one: depth.

Draw a short line from the ear toward the corner of the mouth, just under the cheekbone, then blend upward with the dome brush. Keep the color away from the center of the cheek.

“Shadow should look like a natural hollow, not a stripe.”

Next comes warmth: tap blush on the apples and drift it toward the temple. Skip the bridge of the nose unless you like a sun-kissed look.

Zone three: light.

Dot highlighter along the brow bone, the top of the cheekbone and the Cupid's bow. Press with a fingertip rather than rubbing, which lifts the color you just placed.
