Brand guidelines

Slow, organic skincare for every day.

Balm is luxury organic skincare and sculpting colour, made slowly from botanicals and cold-pressed oils. This guide keeps every page, label and photograph rich, quiet and sure of itself.

Logo

Logo and usage

Four open capitals in one steady stroke, with a small leaf in place of the crossbar of the A and a long glide beneath: the balm drawn across skin. The leaf and the glide take the accent; the letters take the text colour.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter B on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 88 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the leaf and glide in the accent colour or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Use the round leaf mark for favicons, seals and packaging lids.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not move the leaf or straighten the glide.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Plain-spoken luxury

Refined, never precious. Say what it is and how it feels, in few words.

Ingredients first

Lead with the botanical, the oil, the origin. Proof comes from the formula, not from volume.

Unhurried and tactile

Write about touch, scent and ritual. Let sentences breathe and end on a full stop.

We write

  • Cold-pressed oils and organic shea in a cream that melts on contact.
  • Warm between your fingers, then press into skin.
  • Made slowly in small batches.

We avoid

  • Revolutionary anti-ageing breakthrough!
  • Erase wrinkles overnight
  • Chemical-free miracle formula
