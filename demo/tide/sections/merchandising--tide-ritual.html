Ritual by the tide

Rise, refresh, return.

  1. High tide

    Cleanse and protect

    Kelp gel cleanser, then mineral SPF before you step outside.

  2. Midday

    Mist and refresh

    A cooling face mist wherever the sun and salt air reach.

    Restore and rest
