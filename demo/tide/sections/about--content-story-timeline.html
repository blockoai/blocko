Timeline

Four generations of ocean swimmers.

  1. 1920s

    The swimming club

    A grandmother and her friends swim in cold water each morning and credit it for their skin.

  2. 1980s

    A healing-arts career

    Her daughter trains in spa and body therapies and starts to test sea plants in treatments.

  3. 1996

    First formulas on shelves

    The first seaweed skin care line arrives at a small boutique in Los Angeles.

  4. 1998

    The next generation joins

    The founder's daughter comes aboard and helps the brand grow beyond one shop.
