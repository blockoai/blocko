Order matters in a routine. The same products can feel rich or heavy depending on when they go on, so these five steps are laid out from lightest to thickest.

Step one: cleanse.

Massage a gel or milk cleanser over damp skin for sixty seconds, then rinse with lukewarm water. Pat dry rather than rubbing.

Step two: treat.

Press a few drops of serum into slightly damp skin. This is the moment for hydration boosters like seaweed and hyaluronic acid.

“If a product is thin, it goes first. If it is thick, it goes last.”

Step three: moisturize and protect.

Follow with a cream suited to your skin type, and finish mornings with mineral sunscreen. At night, a richer cream or a few drops of face oil seals everything in.

On the body, the same logic applies: exfoliate in the shower, apply oil while skin is wet, then butter on top where you need extra comfort.
