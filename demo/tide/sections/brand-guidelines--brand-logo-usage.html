Logo and usage

Four rounded, single-weight lowercase letters sit above a fine wave line. The crossbar of the t is a small wave and the dot of the i is a droplet, both in the accent colour; the long wave beneath is the shoreline the word rests on.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter d on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 88 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the waves in the accent colour or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not remove or reshape the waves or the droplet.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.
