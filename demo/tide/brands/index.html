Brand guidelines

Skin care that comes in with the sea.

Tide is coastal skincare and body care built around clean water, mineral calm and slow, regular rituals. This guide keeps every page, label and photograph moving at the same easy rhythm.

Logo

Logo and usage

Four rounded, single-weight lowercase letters sit above a fine wave line. The crossbar of the t is a small wave and the dot of the i is a droplet, both in the accent colour; the long wave beneath is the shoreline the word rests on.

Clear space Keep a margin equal to the height of the letter d on every side. Nothing, including text and photo edges, enters it.

Minimum size Wordmark: 88 px wide on screen, 22 mm in print. Mark: 24 px, 8 mm. Below that, use the mark alone.

  • Wordmark SVG
  • Wordmark PNG
  • Wordmark WebP
  • Inverse SVG
  • Inverse PNG
  • Mark SVG
  • Mark PNG
  • Mark WebP

Do

  • Use the primary wordmark on light surfaces and the inverse on dark ones.
  • Keep the waves in the accent colour or in the text colour, never a third colour.
  • Place the logo on calm areas of a photograph with enough contrast.

Don't

  • Do not stretch, rotate or outline the wordmark.
  • Do not remove or reshape the waves or the droplet.
  • Do not set the logo on a busy pattern or low-contrast photograph.

Voice

How we sound

Clear as water

Plain words and short sentences. Say what is inside and what it does, then stop.

Steady, not urgent

Rituals and rhythm, never countdowns. The page should feel like a slow incoming wave.

Fresh and grounded

Sensory, concrete detail: salt, seaweed, mineral, mist. Skip hype and clinical jargon.

We write

  • Sea minerals and kelp, nothing you cannot pronounce.
  • Rinse, pat dry, then smooth on. Morning and night.
  • Light on skin, calm for sensitive days.

We avoid

  • Blast away wrinkles in 24 hours!
  • Revolutionary clinical-grade anti-ageing complex
  • Hurry, only 2 left at this price
