Skin and body questions

Before you pick

Body skin is thicker and loses moisture faster, so body formulas lean on richer oils and butters, while face products use lighter textures and concentrated actives.

Choose the fragrance-free versions of the milk cleanser, hyaluronic serum, barrier cream and body butter, and patch-test any new product first.
